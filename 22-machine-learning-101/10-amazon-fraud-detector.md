# Amazon Fraud Detector

- Amazon Fraud Detector is a fully managed service that uses machine learning to identify potentially fraudulent online activities.
- Input: Historical data.
- Output: Fraud scores.
- Rules can be created based on the fraud scores to take action.
- Model types:
  - Online Fraud: Detects fraud in real-time transactions. Use when you have a small amount of data.
  - Transaction Fraud: Detects fraud in batch transactions. Use when you have a large amount of data.
  - Account Takeover: Detects account takeover attempts.
