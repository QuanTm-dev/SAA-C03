# Amazon Transcribe

- Amazon Transcribe is an automatic speech recognition service that uses machine learning models to convert audio to text.
- Amazon Transcribe offers a variety of features to customize the transcription process, such as:
  - Language identification
  - Custom vocabulary
  - Filters for privacy: Control which words are transcribed and which are not.
  - Etc.
- Use cases:
  - Full text indexing of audio and video content (meaning you can search for specific words in the transcripts).
  - Generate meeting notes.
  - Generate subtitles.
  - Call Analytics: Analyze customer service calls to provide insights such as sentiment, call duration, and more.
