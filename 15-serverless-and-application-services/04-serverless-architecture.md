# Serverless Architecture

- The aim of serverless architecture is to help you manage as few (if any) compute servers as possible.
- In Serverless Architecture, an application is broken into a collection of small and specialised functions.
- The functions run in stateless and ephemeral environment.
- Serverless follows Event-Driven pattern. This mean nothing is running until it's required.
- FaaS products such as Lambda are used for general compute functionality.
- Serverless should use Managed Services like S3 as much as possible instead of reinvent the wheel.
