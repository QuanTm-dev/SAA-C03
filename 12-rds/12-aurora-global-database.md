# Aurora Global Database

- Enable replication across multiple AWS regions using Aurora.
- Great for cross region disaster recovery and buisness continuity.
- Offer low latency for international customers.
- There is ~1s or less replication lag between regions.
- Replication is 1-way from the primary region to the secondary regions.
- Replication happens at the storage level.
- Secondary regions can have 16 replicas.
- Maximum of 5 secondary regions.
- Secondary regions can be promoted to primary in case of disaster recovery.
